🔢 CALCULATION FORMULAS & FIELD REFERENCESEvery computed field — the formula used and every input field it reads from
Monthly P&I Payment
Output: P/I Pmt (terms-pi-payment)
Formula: P × (mr × (1 + mr)n) / ((1 + mr)n − 1)
where mr = Annual Rate ÷ 12
Reads from: OrgL/A mc-principal → P  |  APR mc-rate → annual rate  |  AmTerm mc-term → n (months)
Standard fixed-rate annuity formula. If Rate = 0%, payment = Principal ÷ Term.
Monthly Interest Portion
Output: Monthly Interest (out-interest)
Formula: UPB × (Annual Rate ÷ 12)
Reads from: UPB mc-balance  |  APR mc-rate
Applied to the current unpaid balance (not the original principal). The difference between P/I Payment and Monthly Interest is the Principal Reduction for that period.
Principal Reduction
Output: Principal Reduction (out-principal)
Formula: max(P/I Payment − Monthly Interest, 0)
Derived from: terms-pi-payment and out-interest
On the final payment, principal is set to the exact remaining balance to clear the loan to $0.00 (last-payment rounding correction).
Per Diem Interest Rate
Output: Per Diem (bal-per-diem-display, out-perdiem)
Formula (30/360): UPB × Annual Rate ÷ 360
Formula (Act/365): UPB × Annual Rate ÷ 365
Reads from: UPB mc-balance  |  APR mc-rate  |  Day Count dc-360 / dc-365
Rounded to 4 decimal places. Used in per diem accrual = Per Diem × Days Past Due.
Payoff Amount
Output: Payoff (bal-payoff-display)
Formula: UPB + Int Bal + Late Fee Bal + Adv Bal + Deferred Bal + (Per Diem × Days Past Due)
Reads from: mc-balance  |  bal-int-bal  |  bal-late-fee-balance  |  bal-advance-balance  |  bal-deferred-balance  |  mc-days
Full payoff includes all ledger balances. Reduced Payoff = Payoff − Suspense Balance (mc-suspense).
LTV and Equity
Outputs: LTV (out-ltv), Equity (out-equity)
LTV: (UPB ÷ Property Value) × 100
Equity: Property Value − UPB
Reads from: mc-balance (UPB)  |  mc-prop-value (Property Value)
If LTV is entered directly in mc-ltv that value takes precedence. Equity is the difference between the property value and the current UPB (not the original principal).
Arrears (Auto-Population)
Output: Arrears (bal-arrears)
Formula: Missed Payments × (P/I Payment + Escrow/Month)
Reads from: mc-missed  |  terms-pi-payment  |  escrow monthly (computed from tax/ins/PMI inputs)
Auto-populated only when the field is blank and missed payments > 0. Manual entry overrides auto-population.
Late Charge (Auto-Calculate)
Output: LF Amt (mc-late)
Formula: P/I Payment × 5% (if Days Past Due > Grace Period)
Reads from: terms-pi-payment (base)  |  mc-days (days past due)  |  mc-lf-term (grace period days)  |  mc-late-auto (toggle)
Only fires when the Auto-Calculate Late Charge toggle is ON. Manual entry in LF Amt is used when toggle is OFF.
Modification P/I Payment
Output: Mod P/I (mod-pi-calc)
Formula: Same annuity formula as P/I Payment: ModUPB × (mr × (1 + mr)n) / ((1 + mr)n − 1)
Reads from: Mod Rate mod-rate  |  Mod Term mod-term  |  Mod UPB mod-upb (falls back to mc-balance if Mod UPB is 0)
Computed only when all three mod parameters are entered. Independent of the main loan terms.
Reinstatement Quote
Output: Gross Reinstatement (ri-gross), Net Reinstatement (ri-net)
Gross = Missed P&I + Missed Escrow + Late Charges (new + ledger) + Per Diem Accrual + Escrow Shortage + F/C Costs + Title Fees + Prop Preservation + Advances + NSF
Net = Gross − Suspense Credit − Partial Payment Credit
Reads from: mc-missed, mc-balance, mc-rate, mc-term, mc-principal (for base P/I)  |  ri-lpi, ri-good-through (for per diem days)  |  bal-late-fee-balance (existing ledger)  |  ri-suspense, ri-partial (credits)
Per diem days calculated as LPI → Good-Through if both dates set. LPI pre-populated from Last Paid Date. Suspense pre-populated from Sus Bal.
Amortization Schedule
Output: Full schedule table (amort-body)
Each row: Interest = Balance × (Rate ÷ 12)  |  Principal = min(P/I − Interest, Balance)  |  New Balance = Balance − Principal
Reads from: mc-principal, mc-rate, mc-term for full schedule  |  mc-due-date for start date (falls back to mc-origination + 1 month)  |  mc-acquired-date for Phase I/II delineation
Phase I rows (amber) = Origination → Acquired/Default Date. Phase II rows (green) = Acquired Date → Present. Final payment adjusted to clear balance to exactly $0.00.
